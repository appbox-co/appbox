---
title: App Boost
description: Give one app more memory and CPU by spending spare app slots, without changing your plan.
---

Every app starts with its own share of memory (RAM) and CPU. App Boost raises that share for one app at a time. You pay for it with app slots from your plan that you aren't using, so a busy app can get more room without a plan change.

## How boost works

- Each boost slot adds 0.1× to the app's resources, so 10 boost slots double its RAM and CPU (2.0×).
- An app can go up to 8.0×, which takes 70 boost slots.
- A boosted app uses its own app slots plus its boost slots. An app that needs 1 slot uses 11 with 10 boost slots.
- Boost slots come from your Appbox's free app slots, so the slider stops at the number you have free.

The **Resource Preview** shows the result before you commit to it:

| Tile       | What it shows                                                                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Multiplier | How many times the app's starting resources it gets                                        |
| Slot Cost  | The app's slots plus its boost slots. In the install dialog it also shows your free slots. |
| RAM        | The app's memory after the boost                                                           |
| CPUs       | The app's CPU limit after the boost. `∞` means the app has no CPU limit.                   |

## Boost an app when you install it

1. Open the app in the **App Store** and select **Get**.
2. Choose your Appbox.
3. Drag the **Boost Slots** slider. You can also select the slider and use the arrow keys to move one slot at a time.
4. Check the **Resource Preview**, fill in the rest of the dialog and select **Install**.

## Change the boost on an installed app

1. Go to **Appboxes > Installed Apps** and open the app.
2. In the **App Boost** card, drag the slider to the boost you want.
3. Select **Apply Boost**.

<img
  src="/docs-images/app-boost/app-boost-card.webp"
  alt="App Boost card on an app's page, with the slider at 10 of 70 and the Apply Boost button"
  width="860"
  height="484"
/>

Docker apps get the new resources straight away and keep running. A VPS has to restart before it uses them.

To remove a boost, drag the slider back to 0 and select **Apply Boost**. The boost slots go back to your free slots.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                      | What to check                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The app has no **App Boost** card | Apps installed before App Boost launched use an older resource system and can't be boosted. Reinstall the app to use boost.                                                                 |
| The slider is greyed out          | The app has to be running. You can't change its boost while it's installing, updating, stopped or frozen.                                                                                   |
| The slider stops before 70        | Your Appbox doesn't have that many free app slots. Free some by uninstalling or [freezing](/docs/platform/app-freeze) apps you don't use, or select **Want more boost? Upgrade your plan**. |
