---
title: App Freeze
description: Pause an app you aren't using to get its app slots back, and keep its data for later.
---

Freezing an app stops it and gives its app slots back to your Appbox, including any [boost slots](/docs/platform/app-boost) it had. Its data and settings stay where they are. When you unfreeze it, it starts again as it was, with the same boost.

Use it to try a new app when all your slots are taken, or to put aside an app you only need now and then, without uninstalling it and losing its data.

## Freeze an app

1. Go to **Appboxes > Installed Apps** and open the app.
2. In the **Actions** card, select **Freeze**.
3. Select **Confirm**.

<img
  src="/docs-images/app-freeze/freeze-dialog.webp"
  alt="Freeze dialog saying that freezing the app will stop it and free its app slots, with Cancel and Confirm buttons"
  width="542"

/>

The app's status changes to **frozen**, and its slots count as free on your Appbox straight away.

To freeze several apps at once, tick them in **Installed Apps** and select **Freeze** in the bar that appears. The list doesn't ask you to confirm.

## Unfreeze an app

1. Open the frozen app from **Installed Apps**.
2. In the **Actions** card, select **Unfreeze**.
3. Select **Confirm**.

<img
  src="/docs-images/app-freeze/frozen-app.webp"
  alt="A frozen app's page with the frozen status and the Unfreeze button in the Actions card"
  width="560"
  height="295"
/>

Unfreezing takes the app's slots again, plus its boost slots, so your Appbox needs that many free. The app starts and its address works again within a minute or so.

## While an app is frozen

- It isn't running, so its address doesn't open.
- **Start**, **Stop** and **Restart** are unavailable, and so is its **App Boost** card.
- Its files and settings stay on your Appbox.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                             | What to check                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Freeze** is greyed out                 | Wait until the app finishes installing, updating or restarting. An app whose install failed can't be frozen, so uninstall it instead. |
| **Instance is in a transitional state.** | The app is installing, updating or being removed. Try again when it's done.                                                           |
| Unfreezing fails for lack of slots       | Free enough slots for the app and its boost slots by freezing or uninstalling another app.                                            |
| **Cannot unfreeze due to low quota.**    | Your Appbox has run out of disk space. Delete files to make room, then unfreeze.                                                      |
